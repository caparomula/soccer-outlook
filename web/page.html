Rights notes from Fox Sports, CBS Sports, ESPN and World Soccer Talk. Built by a small open generator on GitHub. Storylines and the forecast are written by Claude, fresh each morning and updated at the scheduled midday and evening builds. Storylines link to their sources. Claude supplies a general overview, league context and a sourced blurb for every rated match. The overview focuses on the current day when possible and looks further ahead when needed. Blurbs with no available matches are hidden; excluded phrases within a relevant blurb are dimmed. Team, league and broadcaster tags show those connections. Claude also rates each upcoming match for popularity (25%), expected gameplay (35%) and competitive impact (40%). Pick scores blend 80% of Claude’s match interest with 20% league priority, adjustable in Lineup. Live now features the highest-scoring available match ESPN confirms is in progress. Up to three other matches are highlighted with their blurbs, selecting from the next 24 hours first and filling spare places from later windows. The featured live match is excluded from those three. Ratings are editorial judgments, not predicted results. Brief context appears with its corresponding schedule window. Later fixtures open by default when fewer than five matches are visible in the next 24 hours. Schedule and data details retain match counts, coverage and the next kickoff for your selection.
