Storylines and the forecast are written by Claude, fresh each morning and updated at the scheduled midday and evening builds. Storylines link to their sources. Claude supplies an interest-rated paragraph for each league with upcoming viewable matches. The page selects one that fits your services and competitions, preferring the next 24 hours and looking further ahead when needed. Blurbs with no available matches are hidden; excluded phrases within a relevant blurb are dimmed. Team, league and broadcaster tags show those connections. Claude also rates each upcoming match for popularity (25%), expected gameplay (35%) and competitive impact (40%). Next up features the best available match within 24 hours, or the next available window. Worth scheduling around requires at least 80/100 on a fixed scale. Ratings are editorial judgments, not predicted results. Brief context appears with its corresponding schedule window. Later fixtures open by default when fewer than five matches are visible in the next 24 hours. Schedule and data details retain match counts, coverage and the next kickoff for your selection.
