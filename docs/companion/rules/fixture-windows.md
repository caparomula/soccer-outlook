# TIME-WINDOW-1: Fixture focus and schedule placement

Project an existing normalized fixture state and explicit times into the
website's focus window and schedule bucket. This extracts `inFocus` and
`bucketOf` together because their boundary checks compose: the inclusive
125-minute focus check takes precedence over the bucket's later checks.

This is a bounded part of TIME-1 and TIME-2 in
[the specification](../specification.md). It does not decode ESPN statuses,
decide whether a result was played, make an old score fresh, extrapolate a clock,
classify source observation age, grant live-only viewing links, or select final
carousel/pick/editorial candidates. P2 and the remaining TIME-2 work stay
incomplete after this family.

## Inputs and outputs

The version 1 operation is `fixture_window`. Every case explicitly supplies:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | Case identity, echoed in the result |
| `now_utc` | The sole evaluation instant |
| `time_zone` | Named device time zone used by the shared sports-day calendar |
| `kickoff_utc` | Absolute fixture instant, including the existing placeholder for a TBD time |
| `time_confirmed` | Whether the source has confirmed the kickoff time |
| `source_state` | Normalized placement phase `pre`, `in`, `post` or `unknown` |
| `received_at` | Receipt instant of the accepted status update, or `null` |

UTC inputs preserve up to six fractional second digits. The required nullable
receipt field distinguishes no recorded receipt from a known timestamp. Invalid
wire shapes, impossible dates, unsupported zones or states, and non-boolean time
confirmation are boundary errors. This operation does not inspect a wall clock.

The subsequent [STATUS-ESPN-1 decoder](espn-status.md) adds the explicit
`unknown` placement seam. It uses the same arithmetic as `pre`, while never
setting `source_in_progress` or `recent_live_receipt`. Unknown source meaning
therefore does not create a source-live claim or remove a valid viewing route.

Each result is `{id, day_index, bucket, in_window, in_focus,
source_in_progress, recent_live_receipt}`. `bucket` is `null` outside the
schedule window; otherwise it is `live`, `earlier`, `today`, `tomorrow`, `day2`,
`day3`, `yesterday`, `dayminus2` or `dayminus3`. `in_window` is exactly whether
the bucket is non-null. `in_focus` is a separate decision and is not implicitly
clipped by the schedule window.

`source_in_progress` means only `source_state == in`. It records what the
accepted observation said, including when that observation is old or its
receipt is unknown. It does not mean the match is freshly confirmed live now.
`recent_live_receipt` is true only for source state `in`, a supplied receipt,
and `0 <= now_utc - received_at < 10 minutes`. A recent download is not proof
that the provider recently observed the score; provider observation freshness
remains a separate fact and future owner.

The independently authored [examples](fixture-window-examples.json) use complete
request and response envelopes. Their expected decisions are literals authored
before core or consumer replacement, not outputs generated by either owner.

## Focus decision

A fixture is in focus only if its source state is not `post` and its kickoff
instant is strictly before `now_utc + 24 hours`. It must also satisfy at least
one of:

1. Kickoff is at or after `now_utc`.
2. Kickoff is at or after `now_utc - 125 minutes`, and either the source state
   is `in` or its time is confirmed.
3. Its source state is `in` and `recent_live_receipt` is true.

The 24-hour upper boundary is exclusive. The 125-minute lower boundary is
inclusive. A scheduled fixture exactly 125 minutes after its confirmed kickoff
is therefore still in focus. One microsecond later it is not, absent another
qualifying condition. This preserves the composed production boundary rather
than silently replacing it with the inner bucket branch's strict inequality.

## Schedule bucket decision

First obtain `day_index` from [TIME-1](sports-day.md), using the supplied zone
and evaluation instant. Local sports days start at 04:00, including across
daylight-saving changes. Apply the following order:

1. Indices outside `-3` through `3` return no bucket, even if the independent
   focus decision is true.
2. A fixture in focus with confirmed time and kickoff at or before evaluation
   enters `live`. This permits a match to cross local midnight or the 04:00
   boundary without immediately leaving the live group.
3. Other negative indices map to `yesterday`, `dayminus2` or `dayminus3`.
4. At index zero, source state `post` enters `earlier`.
5. At index zero, source state `in` enters `live` while evaluation is strictly
   before kickoff plus 155 minutes, or while its live receipt is recent.
   This preserves the existing extra 30-minute allowance within the same
   sports day. It does not extend that allowance across the 04:00 boundary.
6. At index zero, a confirmed-time fixture from kickoff through strictly before
   kickoff plus 125 minutes enters `live`. At or after that limit it enters
   `earlier`, unless an earlier step already selected `live`.
7. Remaining indices zero through three map to `today`, `tomorrow`, `day2` and
   `day3`.

The bucket named `live` is placement, not a source-status assertion. A `pre`
fixture placed there still has `source_in_progress: false`; passing kickoff
does not create a live score, match clock or permission for a live-only link.
Source state `post` remains a normalized producer fact here. Its interpretation
as completed, postponed, cancelled, abandoned or an unfamiliar terminal state
belongs to the future shared status decoder.

## Preserved TBD and surface boundaries

The current website supplies a placeholder instant even when the kickoff time
is not confirmed. This rule preserves that representation and does not invent
a date-only zone policy. A past `pre` placeholder today remains in `today`
without being in focus. A future placeholder inside 24 hours may be in focus.
Neither statement confirms its time or makes it the next confirmed kickoff.

A source-in-progress TBD fixture can enter `live` through the index-zero
155-minute/recent-receipt branch, including when its placeholder lies ahead of
evaluation. Across 04:00, an unconfirmed time cannot use the early confirmed-time
live override and falls into its prior-day bucket. These are preserved legacy
placement quirks, not new assertions about whether play is happening.

Viewer eligibility consumes `in_window`, while focus consumers receive
`in_focus`. The schedule, carousel, picks, league vitals and manual prompts keep
their existing additional candidate rules. In particular, the carousel can
retain a TBD fixture that a pick-candidate predicate omits. This extraction does
not unify those surfaces or change Python's distinct four-hour AI-facts window,
static Eastern-date grouping, final ranking, or editorial expiry.

## Intentional clock corrections

The legacy browser accepted `now` for kickoff arithmetic but used `Date.now()`
for receipt age. This owner uses `now_utc` for both. A pinned preview or another
injected clock therefore produces a deterministic answer independent of the
host's wall clock. A future receipt cannot be recent; negative age no longer
extends a live placement. Exact ten-minute age is already stale under the
existing strict comparison and remains so.

The adapter preserves the accepted receipt instant through redraw, reload or
snapshot reuse when that original fact is available. It must not turn receipt
time into a fabricated provider observation time. Which live response can
replace a fixture, handling of corrections and late replies, and persistence of
receipt facts remain their existing integration owners until separately migrated.

This rule defines the result at an evaluation instant, not a wake-up schedule.
The browser retains its existing minute, navigation, visibility and snapshot
recomputation triggers. This increment does not promise a dedicated timer for
every focus, receipt or sports-day boundary.
