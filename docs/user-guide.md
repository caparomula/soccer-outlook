# Using Soccer Outlook

[Open the site](https://caparomula.github.io/soccer-outlook/) · [Project home](../README.md)

Soccer Outlook helps you find upcoming matches available through your US streaming services and TV channels. Open the **Settings** gear in the bar beneath the title. Its three tabs contain **Lineup** for services, **Leagues** for competitions, and **AI** for headlines, blurbs and imported recommendations. Switching tabs keeps what you have entered; **Done** closes the window.

## Choose what you can watch

In **Settings → Lineup**, tap a broadcaster to enable or disable it; use **Settings → Leagues** for competitions. Enabled and disabled choices have separate areas. You can also drag a pill between them; on a touchscreen, use its dotted handle.

Each group has **Select all** and **Clear all** buttons. Disabled choices are alphabetical. Drag enabled choices to change their order, or focus a pill and press **Alt + ↑** or **Alt + ↓**.

The two orders have different purposes:

- **Broadcasters:** when several selected services carry a match, the first one in your order is shown as the preferred way to watch.
- **Leagues:** higher leagues get a small preference bonus when the page chooses recommendations. The league strip follows this order too.

The schedule includes matches with a listed broadcaster or an established usual home on a selected service. Matches with no known route through your services are omitted. The **Elsewhere in the next 24 hours** section can show notable matches on other services, with listed broadcasters and your competition filters still applied.

### FOX One and TV-provider access

Choose **FOX One (paid subscription)** for a separate FOX One subscription. Having the app on an Apple TV, or signing in through a TV provider, does not establish access to every FOX channel.

For TV-provider access, select the channels you can actually watch through the TV service or app you use: **FOX**, **FS1**, **FS2**, **FOX Deportes** and **Big Ten Network**. They are independent choices: FS1 does not unlock a match listed on FS2. A listing that only says “FOX Sports networks” cannot establish access through an individual channel until the broadcaster confirms which channel carries it.

The generic **Cable or live-TV bundle** option leaves FOX channels to these separate choices. **Fubo** includes its documented Pro-plan channels, the **YouTube TV main and Sports plans** include FOX, FS1, FS2 and Big Ten Network, and **Local channels (antenna)** includes broadcast FOX. These TV packages do not imply a paid FOX One subscription. See [FOX One's subscription information](https://www.fox.com/foxone/) and [Verizon's package-specific Fios lineups](https://www.verizon.com/home/fios-tv/channel-lineup/). A provider name alone is not enough to identify your package or diagnose a playback error.

Channel access describes the broadcast, not a guarantee that every device or app can play it. [FOX notes that some TV programming is unavailable in its apps because of licensing restrictions](https://help.fox.com/s/article/Why-don-t-I-see-a-program-in-the-FOX-Sports-App-that-s-airing-on-one-of-the-FOX-Sports-or-FOX-Entertainment-channels-I-receive-on-my-TV). A Fios lineup can include FS2 without establishing that a particular FS2 match will play in FOX One. If the app is your only way to watch and it denies that match, leave FS2 off until you confirm a usable route; the page cannot check your account's playback authorization.

The older, ambiguous **Fox One** selection is cleared when you first load the updated page. All other saved choices stay intact. Select the paid subscription or your available channels once; the page then remembers those choices normally.

### YouTube TV plans and add-ons

Choose your plan and any add-ons you pay for in **Settings → Lineup**, instead of **Cable or live-TV bundle**. Each choice adds only its included channels. For example, select **YouTube TV (main plan)** and **YouTube TV Sports Plus** if you have both. The main plan is enabled by default; the other YouTube TV plans and add-ons start disabled. Your selections are saved like your other preferences.

| Choice | Soccer coverage included |
| --- | --- |
| **YouTube TV (main plan)** | The former Base Plan: FOX, FS1, FS2, Big Ten Network, ESPN networks and ESPN+, USA Network, NBC/NBCSN, CBS/CBS Sports Network, TNT/TBS/truTV, CNBC, ION, and Spanish channels including TUDN, Univision, UniMás, Telemundo and Galavisión. Universo requires a Spanish package. |
| **YouTube TV Sports Plan** | The core sports channels above, including ESPN+, but without the main plan's CNBC, Galavisión or ION. Sports Plus is a separate purchase; Universo requires a Spanish package. |
| **YouTube TV Sports Plus** | An add-on with FOX Soccer Plus, beIN Sports and beIN Sports XTRA. Select your underlying plan too. |
| **YouTube TV Spanish Plus** | An add-on with ESPN Deportes, FOX Deportes, beIN Sports en Español, TyC Sports and Universo. Select your underlying plan too. |
| **YouTube TV Spanish Plan** | A standalone Spanish subscription: the Spanish Plus channels above, plus TUDN, Univision, UniMás and Galavisión. It does not include Telemundo, English sports channels or ESPN+. |

[ESPN Unlimited is included with the main and Sports plans](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/espn-unlimited-youtube-tv-live-sports/), so ESPN+ matches are available directly in YouTube TV. To watch through the ESPN app instead, the family manager must [link the accounts](https://support.google.com/youtubetv/answer/18116615?hl=en). Carrying a TV channel does not grant a separate Peacock, Paramount+, HBO Max or FOX One subscription. The beIN add-on choices cover their named channels; they do not assume access to beIN Sports Connect overflow streams.

A match listed on Universo requires **Spanish Plus** or the standalone **Spanish Plan** in YouTube TV. A match may still appear through another selected service if it has a separate listing there—for example, Paramount+. The match's service badge identifies the viewing route.

Local affiliates vary by ZIP code. Check [YouTube TV's current plans and local lineup](https://support.google.com/youtubetv/answer/16865391?hl=en), [Sports Plus channels](https://support.google.com/youtubetv/answer/10057534?hl=en), and [Spanish plan options](https://support.google.com/youtubetv/answer/12083189?hl=en) against your subscription. Free broadcasts listed on **YouTube** belong to **Free apps**, separate from paid YouTube TV.

### Quickly hide a league

The bar shows emblems for enabled leagues with matches on your selected services in the displayed schedule, including folded results. It updates when you change services or the schedule's date window changes. A league can remain enabled without appearing in the strip; **Settings → Leagues** lists leagues with fixtures in the fetched schedule, even when none match your services or the displayed days. Tap an emblem to hide that league's matches; tap it again to show them. A hidden league's emblem is dimmed, keeping its colors, with a dashed outline and a small minus badge. Visible leagues have a solid green outline. A short notice offers **Undo**. The strip scrolls sideways if it does not fit.

Hiding keeps the league in the strip while it has matching games and remembers the choice for your next visit. Disabling it in **Settings → Leagues** removes it from the strip. If you enable it again, it starts visible.

With a keyboard, Tab enters the strip; arrow keys, Home and End move between leagues, and Enter or Space toggles the focused league. If ESPN has no emblem for a league, the strip shows a short name instead.

### Focus on one league

Press and hold a league emblem to show only that league, still using your selected broadcasters. Tap or hold the same emblem again to restore your previous selection. You can also choose **Undo** or press **Escape**. Pressing another emblem switches the solo view to that league.

If the solo league's last match leaves the displayed schedule, the page restores your previous selection automatically.

With a keyboard, **Shift + Enter** or **Shift + Space** starts or ends the solo view. Your saved selections and hidden leagues stay intact, even if the solo league was previously hidden. Reloading the page or changing your league or broadcaster selections in **Settings** ends the solo view; simply opening Settings, switching tabs or reordering visible leagues does not.

### Saved preferences and defaults

Services, league selections, ordering, hidden leagues and AI-text visibility are saved in this browser's local storage. They do not synchronize between devices or browsers. Clearing site data removes them; private browsing may discard them when the session ends.

Until you reset, a league you have explicitly enabled or disabled keeps that choice when it returns to a later schedule; a league you have never changed uses its default.

**Reset to defaults**, available on the **Lineup** and **Leagues** tabs, restores the default services and league selections, clears hidden leagues, and resets both orders. It resets both filter groups, whichever of those tabs you use.

The default services, in preference order, are Apple TV, YouTube TV (main plan), HBO Max, Paramount+, Prime Video, Netflix and Disney+. YouTube TV includes its main-plan FOX channels, ESPN coverage and USA Network, so those do not need separate selections. Saved personal choices take precedence over these defaults; use **Reset to defaults** to adopt the current lineup and order.

The default leagues, in preference order, are:

1. Liga MX
2. Champions League
3. Premier League
4. MLS
5. La Liga
6. NWSL
7. Serie A
8. Bundesliga
9. Europa League
10. Concacaf Champions Cup
11. FA Cup
12. Ligue 1
13. Copa del Rey

All other tracked competitions start disabled, including national-team competitions and lower-division domestic leagues: England's Championship, USL Championship and USL League One. You can enable these in **Settings → Leagues**. A US national-team match does not bypass its competition filter. Existing saved selections and orders still apply; choose **Reset to defaults** to adopt this list. Leagues without fixtures in the fetched schedule do not appear in the panel, but their saved choices are kept for when they return.

## Read the schedule

The page shows **today plus three later days**, rather than a rolling 72-hour period. On Thursday, that means Thursday through Sunday. Upcoming day sections are open; today's earlier matches and yesterday's results are folded below them. Empty day sections are omitted.

A day starts at **4 am in your device's time zone**. A 12:30 am Saturday kickoff therefore belongs to Friday's evening. Until 4 am Saturday, the page still calls that period **Today** and labels it with Friday's date.

Kickoff times use your device's time zone and clock format. When your time zone differs from Eastern, the page also shows Eastern time underneath. **TBD** means ESPN has not confirmed a kickoff time. If kickoff passes without a score update, the page says it is awaiting the score; it does not assume the match has started.

The header count and **Your lineup** totals cover the displayed days. **Elsewhere** and the factual summary in the footer use the next 24 hours.

### Featured matches

**Live now** shows the highest-scoring match that passes your filters and that ESPN confirms is in progress. When none is live, **Next up** shows the earliest confirmed kickoff within the displayed days, with a countdown. Simultaneous kickoffs are separated by pick score.

**Top three** chooses the highest-scoring remaining matches across the displayed days, then presents them in kickoff order. It never repeats the top card's match. It can show one or two matches, or disappear when none qualify. With automatic recommendations, a current AI rating on any remaining candidate limits this section to rated matches; otherwise it uses the Outlook score and league priority. While a personal or shared AI response is active, all eligible matches can compete, including newly enabled matches outside that response, which use the site's normal scores.

The site's automatic AI generation supplies ratings, researched headlines and match blurbs. A maintainer can publish a chat response as the shared default, and you can override those recommendations with a personal response through **Settings → AI**, described below. AI text appears only when you enable its display. Match blurbs are available through **Details**, leaving the cards compact. [How recommendations work](scoring.md) explains the scores and configurable alternatives.

### Use your own AI for blurbs

Open **Settings → AI** to use a chat AI you already have access to, such as Copilot, Claude or ChatGPT. This is a copy-and-paste workflow: Soccer Outlook does not sign in to an AI account or send the prompt automatically.

The **Show AI headlines and match blurbs** switch starts off. Turn it on to show available headlines and match blurbs together. Turning it off hides both while retaining AI ratings, rankings, saved responses and preferences. Your explicit choice is remembered in this browser; a different browser starts with AI text off until you enable it there.

1. Choose your services in **Settings → Lineup** and competitions in **Settings → Leagues** first.
2. In **Settings → AI**, describe what you enjoy watching—for example, close games, title races, a favorite club or matches happening this evening.
3. Choose **Create prompt**, then **Copy prompt**, and paste it into your chat. Enable the chat's web search if you want current news. The prompt contains your enabled leagues that are not temporarily hidden, league order and preferences, plus the live and upcoming matches passing your current filters within today and the next three days. With services and leagues selected, you can create a prompt even when there are no matching fixtures, to ask about verified calendar developments such as a break or resumption.
4. The chat is asked to return headline candidates, ratings and blurbs in one formatted JSON code block. Use the block's **Copy** button, if available, and paste the complete response into **AI response**, then choose **Apply response**. The app checks the response before using it; if it reports an error, your previous recommendations stay in place. Ask the chat to correct the response using the original prompt. If you have connected GitHub publishing, this button becomes **Apply & publish**, as explained below.

Qualifying AI headlines appear in a card carousel above the featured matches, with the strongest first. Each card has its league emblem and headline at the top, with the supporting story text directly underneath. The cards share one height so browsing headlines does not move the matches below. Choose a dot underneath the cards to jump to that headline; the highlighted dot shows your current position. You can also swipe horizontally on a phone or other touch screen. With the carousel focused, use the left and right arrow keys, or Home and End to reach the first or last card. Cards never advance automatically.

The chat considers every league in the prompt, including those without fixtures. It is asked to use the strongest researched match angle—players, tactics, rivalries or meaningful stakes—and weave the competition's familiar name naturally into the headline. Routine fixture announcements should be omitted. The app prefers the nearest 24-hour window, then combines 80% AI headline interest with 20% of your saved league priority. A headline must still clear the minimum editorial interest rating of 60 before league preference counts. If none qualifies, the whole headline area is omitted, without a placeholder or overview paragraph.

Match-specific blurbs and their sources appear in the match's **Details** preview or dialog, for both featured cards and schedule rows. The prompt asks for concrete angles about players, coaches, tactics, rivalries or competitive stakes, supported by current reporting when web search is available. It asks the chat to avoid general football news, generic filler and repeating the schedule's times, venues or channels. A match still receives a rating when there is no useful supported angle; its blurb can be empty.

Recommendations still follow your services and league filters. A match headline appears only while every match it references remains viewable and live or upcoming, with an unchanged kickoff. Completed matches do not remain eligible for round summaries. A sourced calendar notice can appear without a listed match, but its league must remain enabled and visible, and at least one service must be selected. It also needs a verified start and end for its upcoming or ongoing event: Saturday's round is ranked as Saturday news, and a notice disappears when its event ends. A lack of fixtures is not proof of a break; claims that a competition starts or resumes need supporting reporting. The AI cannot add fixtures, change kickoff times or scores, or make a match available on another service.

**Imported rating influence** sets the balance between your AI's rating and the site's Outlook score for your personally imported matches. At 100%, match interest comes entirely from the imported rating; at 0%, it uses the Outlook score. Your league priority still contributes to the final pick score. Changing this slider takes effect without another chat request. Shared recommendations use the blend saved by their publisher; other matches keep the site's automatic scoring.

Your preferences and accepted response are saved in this browser. Recommendations expire **24 hours after the prompt was created**; individual headlines may expire sooner. Pasting the same response again does not refresh them. Use **Create prompt** and run it in your chat again for a new schedule, different interests or updated writing instructions; existing imports are not rewritten. After changing filters, existing matching recommendations can still be used; create another prompt to cover newly included matches or leagues.

Older saved responses keep their ratings and match blurbs until the original expiry, but their overview paragraphs no longer appear at the top or prevent shared headlines from appearing. Calendar notices without event dates are hidden; the AI settings explain how to update them. Choose **Create prompt** and run a fresh response to get the revised headline instructions and dated calendar coverage. Existing prose is not automatically rewritten. Older overview-format prompts are retired; your preferences and accepted ratings stay intact. If your chat returns the old overview format for a new prompt, the app asks you to correct the response instead of accepting it. It does not turn paragraphs into headlines automatically.

Choose **Restore site recommendations** to remove your personal import and return to available shared recommendations, then the site's automatic ratings where needed. This keeps your lineup and preference text and does not remove published defaults. A personal response survives page refreshes and can make headlines differ between devices; use this option in the browser showing **Your AI headline** to return to the published recommendations. Personal ratings take precedence, and a current-format personal response's intentionally empty headline list keeps the headline area empty. Applying a response without a publishing connection changes only this browser. Clearing site data removes local preferences and imports, and private browsing may not retain them. The prompt becomes visible to your chosen AI service when you paste it there. Source links supplied by a chat are references, not independent verification by Soccer Outlook.

### Publish recommendations for everyone

This optional control is for maintainers with write access to the Soccer Outlook repository. Other visitors can use shared recommendations without signing in. Publishing uses a GitHub token; simply being signed into github.com does not connect the app.

1. Open [GitHub's fine-grained token form](https://github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new?name=Soccer%20Outlook%20publishing&target_name=caparomula&expires_in=30&contents=write). Alternatively, go to **GitHub Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens → Generate new token**.
2. Choose **caparomula** as the resource owner, set an expiration, and select **Only select repositories → soccer-outlook**. Under repository permissions, grant **Contents: Read and write**. No Actions, Workflows or administration permission is needed. The link prefills some fields; check the repository selection yourself.
3. In **Settings → AI → Publish site defaults**, enter the token and choose **Connect GitHub**. Paste it only into this publishing control, never into your AI chat or prompt. Connecting checks repository write access without publishing anything. The token stays only in this tab's memory; **Disconnect GitHub** or reloading forgets it.
4. Review the response you want everyone to see, then use **Apply & publish**. To publish an already accepted response or retry after a publishing failure, use **Publish saved response**.

Publishing creates a GitHub commit and starts the normal site deployment. A successful save means the commit was accepted; visitors receive the new defaults after deployment finishes. A publishing failure leaves your personal response available so you can retry.

The public file contains the accepted recommendations and the fixture information needed to match them to the schedule. It excludes the token, your preference text, full prompt and broadcaster selections. The response itself is public, including any preferences the AI has expressed in its prose. Each visitor's filters still control what appears, and personal imports take precedence. Publishing does not restart the 24-hour lifetime or a headline's earlier expiry.

### Opening a match on a streaming service

Hover over a network pill, or click or tap it, to see which of your selected services carry that network. With a keyboard, focus the pill and press Enter or Space. The list follows your service preference order. Underlined provider names open a verified viewing destination in a new tab; names without a supported link remain plain text. Press Escape or click outside to dismiss the list.

Some verified broadcaster listings supply an exact match page for Apple TV, ESPN, ViX, Peacock or beIN Sports; you may still need to sign in and select playback. Each link must match both the network and the provider it names.

For **YouTube TV**, direct links are available only for matches whose watch URL has been supplied and matched to the fixture and channel. These links appear only while ESPN reports the match as live: a YouTube TV watch URL can identify a live channel, which may show another program before or after the match. Soccer Outlook cannot automatically discover YouTube TV's program identifiers from public schedules, so most matches have no direct YouTube TV link.

A compatible verified ESPN player link may be offered instead; watching there may require [linking your YouTube TV and ESPN accounts](https://support.google.com/youtubetv/answer/18116615?hl=en). Hover over a provider link to see its destination. Without a supported link, [search YouTube TV](https://support.google.com/youtubetv/answer/7067974?hl=en) for either team or the listed channel.

Links open on the device you are using. Whether they launch an installed app depends on your browser and device; they do not control a separate television. A link does not guarantee playback or replay availability: your service account, package and location still determine access. Matches without a supported viewing link retain their broadcaster information and source links in **Details**.

### What the colors and symbols mean

| On the page | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Up to six golden dots | The pick score ÷ 100 × 6, rounded to the nearest whole number; 80.5/100 gives five dots. A score rounding to zero dots shows `0/6`. Hover for the rating breakdown, or click or tap to open it in a dialog. These rate recommended interest, not a predicted result. |
| Up to five small squares beside a team | Recent form: green is a win, grey a draw, red a loss. The tooltip shows the W/D/L sequence. |
| A record such as `6–2–1` | Season wins, draws and losses, in that order. |
| Colored broadcaster pill | That channel is available through one of your selected services. |
| Grey broadcaster pill | That channel is outside your selected services; another listed channel may still carry the match for you. |
| Dashed broadcaster pill / **usually** | The competition's established usual coverage, without a confirmed match listing yet. |
| Two colored strips at the bottom of a featured card | The home and away teams' colors. They are decorative, not another rating. |

League emblems identify competitions; badges beside team names identify the teams. Dark emblems and badges receive a contrast adjustment for the dark theme, while already bright images retain their colors.

Network pills stack on the right when a match card has enough width. In narrower cards, they run along the bottom and wrap as needed. For example, **USA Network** can reveal **YouTube TV (main plan)** in its provider list. A service that is also the broadcaster, such as **Paramount+**, has just one pill. Changing your service preferences updates the pill colors, provider lists and viewing links.

### Team vitals

Tap or click a team's name or badge in a schedule row, **Live now / Next up**, or **Top three** to open its team sheet. With a keyboard, focus the team and press Enter. Close the sheet with **Close**, Escape, or a tap outside it.

The sheet shows available records and recent form immediately, then loads the team's home league, nickname, home ground and its city from ESPN. National teams are not assigned a permanent home ground. Domestic and cup records are labelled separately. **Leading contributors** lists up to three players with the most combined goals and assists in the displayed season and competition; it is a statistical selection, not an AI assessment of star quality. Links open the full squad, team statistics and ESPN team page.

Only available facts are shown. Coach names are omitted because ESPN's roster feed can retain former coaches. If a lookup fails, the schedule's existing team facts remain available and **Try again** retries the lookup. Team profiles load only when opened and are cached in this browser for six hours, including across page reloads. Without JavaScript, team links open ESPN directly.

### League vitals

Click or tap a league emblem on a schedule row, **Live now / Next up**, or **Top three** to open a league sheet. With a keyboard, focus the emblem and press Enter or Space. The sheet describes the competition, shows up to three live or upcoming matches on your selected services, and lists the top five teams in each published standings group. A **Full league table** link opens the complete standings on the page; **Competition on ESPN** opens ESPN's league page.

Fixture counts cover the loaded schedule, not the whole season. Standings come from the page's latest build; live scores update while the sheet is open. Cups and friendlies may have no standings table. Missing information is left out or labelled unavailable. Close with **Close**, Escape, or a tap outside the dialog. The emblems in the filter bar and Settings remain filter controls.

### Match details

Hover over **Details** for a floating preview. Click, tap or press Enter to open a dialog. Close it with **Close**, Escape, or a click outside it. Neither view expands the schedule row.

Details can include season records, leading scorers, venue information and links, depending on what ESPN supplies. When AI text is enabled, the match's available story and sources appear here too. Featured cards show extra match facts and links directly. **Add to calendar** opens a Google Calendar event draft for a confirmed kickoff; it is unavailable while the time is TBD. The event names the broadcast outlet rather than assuming which subscription you use. **League table** opens the page's standings. The schedule remains usable when an optional fact or image is missing.

Hover over a match's **golden dots** for the rating breakdown. Click or tap them to keep it open in a dialog; keyboard users can focus the dots and press Enter or Space. Automatic AI ratings show **Popularity**, **Expected gameplay** and **Competitive impact**. Outlook inputs show **Match stature**, **Evenness**, **Competitive stakes**, **TV reach** and **Expected goals**. Each available component has a number from 0 to 100 and up to six colored dots, using the same proportional rounding as the pick score. Missing inputs say **Not available**, without dots. Personal or shared AI imports provide only a total rating, so the breakdown does not invent their missing components or substitute those from another AI response.

The popup includes the weights used to blend the score. Component rows stay in the popup or dialog; the match card keeps its overall golden dots. Popularity and TV reach are assessments of appeal and distribution, not measured attendance or viewership. See [How matches are ranked](scoring.md) for the formulas.

For supported competitions, the build checks broadcasters' public schedules, including possible simulcasts missing from ESPN. A matching live broadcast replaces **usually** with the listed channel or service. **Details** links to the evidence and shows when each channel was verified. If no matching listing can be confirmed, the usual coverage remains, and Details explains whether the schedule was checked without a match or could not be verified automatically. A missing listing is not proof that a match will be unavailable. During a source outage, a previously verified listing may be reused for at most six hours and is identified as saved evidence.

## Freshness and missing matches

Full builds run three times a day, with lighter fixture and broadcast refreshes scheduled every half hour. Open tabs check for those updates every five minutes. GitHub can delay a scheduled build. Live scores are checked roughly once a minute while the tab is visible and a match is near kickoff or in progress. Returning to a tab also checks unfinished matches that kicked off within the past 30 hours, so a score seen earlier can catch up to the final result. If a request fails, the last displayed score remains until a later successful check. Live-score polling and schedule updates are separate. A successful schedule update brings in new fixtures, kickoff changes and broadcaster assignments without reloading your preferences or AI response. An incomplete or failed update leaves the current schedule in place. After an incompatible code update, the data-details footer asks you to reload the page. Standings refresh with the full builds.

Open **Schedule and data details** in the footer for the build time, latest score check, coverage counts and any incomplete-data notice. The page warns when its build is more than 30 hours old.

If a match is missing, check **Settings → Leagues** and the league strip, check your services in **Settings → Lineup**, and check whether it falls within the displayed days. A known league rights deal does not always establish coverage for every fixture. Broadcast assignments can change, so confirm the listing in the broadcaster's app before kickoff.

For maintainers, [Development and maintenance](development.md) explains the data sources, update schedule and coverage report.
